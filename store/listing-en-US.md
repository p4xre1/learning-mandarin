## Full description

Míngdào helps you build practical Mandarin through a clear, welcoming learning
path.

Explore structured HSK study guides, review sourced vocabulary with
tone-marked pinyin and traditional forms, practise beginner sentence patterns,
and discover the cultures and places behind the language.

Features:

- 1,750 sourced vocabulary entries across HSK study packs
- Simplified and traditional Chinese
- Tone-marked Hanyu Pinyin
- English CC-CEDICT definitions
- English, Modern Standard Arabic, and Moroccan Darija interface
- Flashcards and local review scheduling
- Real study time, streak, progress, and XP
- Offline rule-based sentence corrections
- Mandarin culture, food, city, travel, and tradition guides
- Optional local study reminders
- Encrypted local profile with PIN protection
- Local progress export

Privacy by design:

- No account required
- No advertising
- No analytics or tracking SDK
- No cloud profile
- No remote database
- Learning progress stays on your device
- Bundled fonts and photography avoid third-party media requests

The first interactive lesson is available now. Remaining curriculum units are
clearly identified as study guides and are not represented as completed
interactive lessons.
